\caption{\method. A count-adapted video transformer reads the video once at
frame strides 2, 4 and 8 and at two depths; overlapping clips are averaged into
token streams on a shared frame grid, pooled over space, fused per cell with a
learned dataset token, processed by a temporal encoder and decoded into an event
density whose sum is the count. Shifting the pyramid by one level gives the exact
tokens of the video played twice as fast, used as training augmentation and as
a second test-time view. In a second stage the decoder is frozen and a
LoRA-tuned language model reads a description of the repeated action, written
by a frozen MLLM, and the decoder's temporal tokens, given twice; it answers the
count as text (auxiliary loss), and its hidden states are added back to the
decoder features through a zero-initialised projection.}
