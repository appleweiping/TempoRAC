\caption{\method. A count-adapted video transformer reads the video once at
frame strides 2, 4 and 8 and at two depths; overlapping clips are averaged into
token streams on a shared frame grid, pooled over space, fused per cell with a
learned dataset token, processed by a temporal encoder and decoded into an event
density whose sum is a count. Shifting the pyramid by one level gives the exact
tokens of the video played twice as fast, used as training augmentation and as a
second view at test time. The two views are right in different tempo ranges, and
which one depends on the action: a frozen MLLM describes the repeated action and
one repetition, and the views are weighted by the vote of the held-out videos with
the most similar descriptions, each voting for the view that was right for it.}
