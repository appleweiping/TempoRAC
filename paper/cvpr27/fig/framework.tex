\caption{\method. A frozen video transformer reads the video once at frame strides
2, 4 and 8 and at two depths; overlapping clips are averaged into token streams on
a shared frame grid, pooled over space, fused per cell with a learned dataset
token, processed by a temporal encoder and decoded into an event density whose sum
is the count. Shifting the pyramid by one level gives the exact tokens of the
video played twice as fast, used as training augmentation and as a second view at
test time; the reported count averages the two views. One decoder is trained on
the union of RepCount-A, UCFRep and Countix.}
