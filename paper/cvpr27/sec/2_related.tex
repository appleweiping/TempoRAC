\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Repetition counting.}
Early approaches estimated a dominant frequency from motion signals or learned
live counters on short periodic clips~\cite{levy2015live,runia2018realworld}.
RepNet~\cite{dwibedi2020repnet} introduced the temporal self-similarity matrix
(TSM) as a class-agnostic bottleneck and predicts a per-frame period length and
periodicity; at test time it scans several frame strides and keeps the stride
with the highest periodicity score. Zhang~\etal~\cite{zhang2020context} refine
coarse cycle estimates in a context-aware, scale-insensitive manner, and
Sight\&Sound~\cite{zhang2021sight} adds audio. TransRAC~\cite{hu2022transrac}
released RepCount, a benchmark with fine-grained cycle annotations and large
period variation, and encodes multi-scale TSMs with a transformer to regress a
density map. Subsequent methods improved motion features~\cite{li2024mfl},
resolution~\cite{li2023fullres}, query design~\cite{ma2026detrc}, and exemplar-based
correspondence: ESCounts~\cite{sinha2024escounts} trains a decoder on frozen
masked-autoencoder video tokens with repetition exemplars and counts
exemplar-free at test time.
Pose-based counters exploit salient body poses~\cite{yao2025poserac} or
period-adaptive self-supervision~\cite{gao2026pams}; CountLLM~\cite{yao2025countllm}
leverages a large language model and web-scale video-text pre-training.
These works fix the temporal sampling of the input---a uniform 64-frame
resampling in TransRAC, a single stride in ESCounts---and adapt to tempo only
implicitly or through a global choice.

\paragraph{Multi-scale and adaptive temporal processing.}
Multi-rate inputs are standard in action recognition, \eg~two-pathway
SlowFast networks~\cite{feichtenhofer2019slowfast} and temporal pyramids, but
they fuse the pathways with fixed weights. Input-adaptive computation and
mixture-of-experts layers~\cite{shazeer2017moe} select capacity per token; in
counting, scale-aware routing is well studied for crowd density estimation in
images. Our design applies the routing idea to the temporal sampling rate itself,
conditioned on an explicit, parameter-free local periodicity cue, and decodes
the routed mixture once.

\paragraph{Local periodicity estimation.}
Windowed autocorrelation and short-time spectra are classical tools for
non-stationary periodic signals~\cite{welch1967fft,griffin1984stft}; RepNet's
TSM and its banded variants are learned counterparts. We use a masked local
autocorrelation of learned features (a tempogram) not as a counter but as the
evidence that tells the model which temporal resolution to trust at each
moment.
